V20 — scalable central storage fix

- Prevents a single document from filling up as employees/attendance/sales grow.
- Face enrollment stores each employee's face samples in its own document.
- Attendance and sales are stored one record per document.

- Preserved existing Firebase Auth, DTR, payroll, sales, dashboard, and face recognition flows.
